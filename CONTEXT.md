# TrueScore — domain & architecture context

TrueScore replaces inflated star averages with a score derived only from reviews
that look trustworthy. A browser **extension** injects scores onto Google Maps,
Letterboxd, Goodreads, Amazon, Booking, etc.; a **web** app (Bun on Hetzner)
does the same for a pasted Google Maps link. Three packages: `extension`, `web`,
and `gmaps-shared`.

## Domain vocabulary

- **Item** — whatever a site's reviews are about: a Place, a product, a book, a film, a hotel. _Avoid_: "product" or "place" as the generic word.
- **Place** — a Google Maps location, identified by a **featureId** (`0x…:0x…` hex pair) parsed from the URL.
- **Review** — one Google review: `stars`, `reviewerReviewCount`, `timestamp`, `text`.
- **Trust filtering** — a review counts only if its author is **trusted**: `reviewerReviewCount >= TRUSTED_MIN_REVIEWS` (3). Filtering out drive-by/low-history reviewers is the whole premise.
- **Score / scorePct** — net polarity over *trusted* reviews: 5★ → +1, 1★ → −1, else 0, averaged and ×100 (`statsForReviews`).
- **Histogram** — the `[5★,4★,3★,2★,1★]` counts; `overallPct` and the integer `overallScore` are derived from it.
- **relevant / newest** — the two Google sort orders scored in parallel; pagination stops when `scorePct` **stabilizes** (within 1% after ≥2 pages).
- **Chip** (topic chip) — a Google Maps topic (e.g. "light show", "elevator") with a `token`; TrueScore re-scores the reviews under each chip. *(Formerly named `Highlight` — renamed to end the collision below.)*
- **Summary** — LLM output for a place (gemini, openai, or deepseek; see packages/web/llm.ts): a prose **verdict**, a list of **SummaryHighlight**s, **valueForMoney** (1–5, unset when fewer than two reviews judge the price), and **items** (praised dishes/menu terms). The model reads a **Subject** (`web/summary-subject.ts`): place name, review texts, and — when Google shows one — the **RemovedReviews** takedown notice, which `removalNote` turns into a prompt paragraph: weigh the set as survivor-only (takedowns are business-requested and skew negative), check the surviving reviews for corroboration, and mention the removal only when they corroborate it — the UI already shows Google's banner. The extension ships the notice in the body; the web caller gets it from the cached preview meta.
- **SummaryHighlight** — one verdict bullet: `{ text, sentiment }`. Distinct from **Chip** — both used to be called "highlight".
- **items** (dish chips) — short praised dish/menu terms from the Summary, auto-scored via a **Search** each and ranked in the topic chips' row, one naming a topic pooled into it (both review sets, each review once); clicking one opens that search.
- **Stance** — what one review says about a topic (a Chip's label, a Search's query): praise, complain, mixed (both, or a mention without a verdict) or off (not about it at all). Read by **Jev**, TypeSafe's decision model, on the server (`web/jev.ts`) and counted over the trusted reviews a TrueScore counts; a Chip or Search shows "▲praise ▼complain" in place of its star share, each count filters the reviews behind it, and each review listed under it is marked ▲ ▼ ● (mixed) or ○ (off) by what it says, or, by an untrusted author (never read), "untrusted · N reviews" (`reviewMark`). Unread (no `TYPESAFE_API_KEY`, or Jev down), everything shows the star share as before. An Ask's Searches get the same read against the question: yes / no / unclear / doesn't say. A comment's Stance on an **Option** is the same read (praise for, complain against), taken as an answer to the Thread's question and with the comment it replies to, so a bare "this" carries its parent's stance.
- **Receipt** — a SummaryHighlight's support: how many of the summarized reviews make its point (Jev, one yes/no per review and bullet), with those reviews to click through. A summary streams in as the model writes it, its bullets dim until they're checked; a bullet fewer than two reviews make then folds away, and a better alternative stays only if two reviews naming it would rather have it and more would than wouldn't — every one that passes for a Place, the most preferred for a product. One policy for both packages (`gmaps-shared/receipts.ts`), over a support-reader port: Jev in-process on the server, the server's endpoints from the extension.
- **Search** — arbitrary review-text search within an Item, run by the user or by the model during an **Ask**. A Gmail-style ` OR ` operator (`parseOrQuery`, any case) splits the query into terms; each is searched separately and the matches are unioned (dedup by `reviewId`). It spans every review of the Item, except on sites that offer no way to reach them all, where it covers only the reviews already fetched and says how many. _Avoid_: label search (extension UI copy only).
- **Ask** — a free-form question about an Item, or about a Chip's or Search's reviews, answered by the LLM from a **Sample**. When the Sample doesn't settle it, the model runs its own Searches over every review of the Item before answering — offered only where a Search reaches reviews the Sample lacks. Its output is the **Answer**. Extension Asks run on the popup's model, each Search going through that site's own review search. A Thread is asked from its Tally's drawer, on the server like the Tally: the model reads every comment the page loaded (no Sample, no Searches), cites the comments its Answer rests on, and takes the Thread's earlier questions and Answers along, so a follow-up reads as one; the last ten are kept for the Thread.
- **Sample** — the reviews an Ask starts from: for a Place, whatever was collected before the score stabilized — never its full review set; for a product, its newest reviews up to a few thousand — all of them, where the site hands them all over — and its summary reads the same set; or the Chip/Search subset being asked about.
- **Thread** — a Reddit post and every comment under it. _Avoid_: post (only its top text), discussion.
- **Option** — one thing a Thread's commenters put forward, or argue against, as an answer to its question, whatever that asks for: a recommendation, a store when it asks where to buy, or the most dangerous submission when that's what it asks. It can hold the narrower Options named under it (an instructor's courses, a brand's models), and a Stance on one counts for the Option holding it too. A reply to a comment naming several of one Option's narrower Options speaks of the Option holding them, not of each; an answer to the post's own list as a whole ("none of those", "all of the above") speaks of each Option it lists. One with no name of its own, a way of doing it ("make them at home" where the post asks for a place), is put in each commenter's own words, so Jev is asked which comments speak of it rather than its name searched for. _Avoid_: recommendation (that's a praise Stance), pick (taken by best-ratio and Goodreads picks), candidate.
- **Tally** — a Thread's Options ranked by net people: each commenter counts in full for every Option they speak of, for, against, or neither when their comments disagree; switchable to the net upvotes of those comments, and to the narrower Options ranked on their own (a brand's models rather than the brand; an Option holding none stands as it is). Bots, deleted or removed comments, and comments voted to 0 or below don't count. It reads only the comments the page has loaded, never the whole Thread, and folds Options fewer than two people name into one row. _Avoid_: vote (on Reddit, an upvote), ranking.

## Architecture vocabulary

Uses the deepening glossary — *module, interface, seam, adapter, deep*.

- **`gmaps-shared`** — the deep module holding everything a Google-side or contract change should touch in one place: the RPC schema readers, the scoring math, the review-collection loop, and the wire contract — including its client side (fetch-with-retry and the NDJSON stream reader both clients use against `/api/*`). Storage, the Google-side **Transport** adapters, DOM, and routing deliberately stay in `web`/`extension` — all but the motion every client shares: the topic row's chips (`chip-row.ts`, beside their pooling and order) and a streamed summary's bullets (`summary-draw.ts`). Each Provider's settings (model, thinking or effort, the extension's output cap) are `models.ts`'s, `modelCall` turning a key into a configured model call; the server adds only its keys and explicit prompt caching (its calls set their own output caps), the extension the popup's key and effort and Anthropic's direct-browser-access header. Every summary's model call, both packages', and the server's Thread Ask, whole or streamed, go through `write.ts`, which also owns the salvage of a structured reply cut off at the token cap (each caller hands it its own salvage).
- **Review-collection loop** (`gmaps-shared/collect.ts`) — `collectPaged`/`collectSort`/`collectToken`/`collectSearchTerms`: paginate a cursor URL → parse → dedup by `reviewId` → stop (no cursor / maxPages / `onPage` 'stop' / stabilized / aborted). The kernel both packages used to duplicate. `collectSearchTerms` layers the OR-search fan-out on top — one paged search per term, run in parallel and unioned by `reviewId` — so the web and extension search paths don't each re-derive it. Each package hands it a per-term cache (a week in the server's sqlite, `cache.terms`; an hour in memory in the extension, `createTermCache`), so a query only searches the terms no recent query already did.
- **Transport** (the seam) — `(url, { signal? }) => Promise<string>`, injected into the loop. Two **adapters**: `googleFetch` (proxy + cookies + retry) on the server, a tab-session `fetch` in the extension. The extension layers abort/pause/resume, a page-1 live-head reconcile, and per-period bucketing on top via `onPage` — that orchestration stays per-package.
- **Wire contract** (`gmaps-shared/wire.ts`) — the single source of truth for every `/api/*` request body, JSON response, and NDJSON stream-event shape, plus the canonical `Provider` / `ReasoningEffort` enums and `LlmOverrides` (imported by `web/llm.ts` and the extension's `config.ts`, not re-declared). The server is the producer: JSON returns use `satisfies`, request handlers parse `as <Request>`. The web client and the extension both build bodies `satisfies <Request>`, gate-checked by `tsc` on every package via `check` (the extension gained a `tsconfig` + `@types/chrome`; it holds `strict` but not `noUncheckedIndexedAccess` — its scripts are DOM scrapers where a bad index is a visible failure, not a silent wrong score; see `packages/extension/tsconfig.json`).
- **checked Summary** (`web/checked-summary.ts`) — `checkedSummary`: the one path behind the server's three summary routes (a Place's, a Chip's, a Search's). It owns the cache decision (a kept Summary is served unless forced), the Receipt backfill of one kept before Jev, the drafts, the Receipts of a fresh one, and a Place's late aliases, each Summary kept in its own slot so a Chip's or a Search's never lands in the Place's. The routes keep only body parsing and JSON-vs-stream. Tests run it, and the Tally, on a fake model (`llm.setModel`, `web/fake-model.ts`).
- **summary-parse** (`web/summary-parse.ts`) — pure salvage + cleanup of structured-summary LLM text (truncated-JSON recovery, item hygiene), split from `llm.ts` so its fragile bits are tested through fixture strings (`summary-parse.test.ts`) instead of a live model.
- **markdown** (`gmaps-shared/src/markdown.ts`) — the shared LLM-markdown renderer (`mdInline`/`mdToHtml`): pure string→html, deliberately hand-rolled (not a library) to tame the malformed markdown models emit — stray/odd `**`, `__bold__`, snake_case `_` — where a spec parser would leave literal markers or need XSS sanitizing. Both packages consume it and keep their own DOM sink (`renderMarkdown`: extension `innerHTML`, web `createContextualFragment`). Tested in `markdown.test.ts`.
- **Score grid** (`extension/src/shared/score-grid.ts`) — `setupScoreGrid`: the shared list behaviour — badge every card with its net score, then re-rank the container by `data-nps` (scored desc, unscored last), progressively as scores land. Ranking is CSS-order-only by default (`orderByCssBand`) so re-rendering hosts never fight it; `data-nps` rides on the badge so rank survives wrapper recreation. Owns the rAF coalescing and the debounced `MutationObserver`; the per-site parts are injected slots (`scoreForCard`, `placeBadge`, `discover`, `rankableChild`, `applyOrder`) with batteries-included helpers (`structuralContainers` / `containersBySelector`; `orderByCssBand` / `orderByCssImportant` / opt-in `orderByAppend`). Each ranking also tints its **best-ratio picks** (`markBestRatios`: a displayed ratio ≥1 point above every card ranked higher) and `]`/`[` cycle them (`cycleBestRatios`) — both shared with Amazon search. Airbnb search gets star shares from its smaller PDP sections request, falling back to a listing page if that private query changes. On the first search page, `airbnb-search-extra.ts` requests 36 search results, appends linked summaries for the second 18, and adjusts pagination so the next click starts at result 37.
- **Score badge** — `renderScoreBadge` (in `score-grid.ts`): the compact `score (nps%)` span placed beside a host rating on a product card, shared across the score grids.
- **Airbnb review summary** (`extension/src/sites/airbnb-reviews.ts`) — the room page's review panel samples up to 50 lowest-rated and 50 most relevant reviews with two parallel requests, deduplicates them, and feeds their star-labeled text into the shared praised/complaints summary widget. The panel reports how many written reviews it actually read.
- **Ask run** (`extension/src/shared/review-ask.ts`) — `askReviews`: every extension Ask, Maps' and the other sites', over a chat transport the page passes in (the server's `/api/ask` for Maps, the popup's model in-process elsewhere). It owns Jev's read of each Search's matches, the abort once the user leaves, the Recent questions (a kept Answer replays with its Searches), the popup key's daily count, and the one-pass Ask where a page can't Search. Pages keep their own painter (Maps its "Ask again" note).
- **Review panel** (`extension/src/shared/review-panel.ts`) — `buildReviewPanel`: a retail product page's review contents, from one description of its Item's reviews (`ItemReviews`: every review it downloads and how many that can be, the newest for the recent gauge, the total, how one reads as text and as a card, its own one-term search, a cache key). The panel owns the rest: the Sample (newest `SAMPLE_MAX`, each text once), the Search box (the site's own search past what it downloads, else the reviews in hand), when an Ask may Search (only where that reaches reviews the Sample lacks), the matches' summarize widget, quote cards, the recent gauge with its adjusted and analyzed stats (the site's own stats joining its row), and the Summary. IKEA, Decathlon, dm/DocMorris, OTTO, Printful, Etsy, BJJ Fanatics, Progress and Amazon use it; their own parts (a bespoke gauge, insights, variations, topics) sit in the island beside it. Contents only: each site keeps its lifecycle (ADR-0001).
- **Score island** (`extension/src/shared/score-island.ts`) — the dark-glass `.ars-wrapper` PDP panel. `createIslandShell` (the "Review Intelligence" header shell) is shared by all ten retail PDPs; `buildGauge` (the **gauge** — a `% positive on this item` bar + truescore/reviews stats) is the byte-identical headline used by `etsy-pdp` + `aliexpress-pdp` (which also move to `setupSpaInjector`) and `progress-pdp`. Sites compose their own sections into the shell beside the review panel — a variation card, topics, insights, or a bespoke gauge (amazon its live walk's, bjjfanatics its clickable one, etsy and progress `buildGauge`; the review panel draws the recent gauge for IKEA, Decathlon and the BazaarVoice island dm, DocMorris and Rossmann share, `shared/bazaarvoice.ts`). bjjfanatics and progress-pdp fetch their reviews through the shared Stamped client, `shared/stamped.ts`; its `cardScores` scores their list cards without a request per card — one badges call per 100 cards gives each count and exact average, a daily census of the store's rare 1★–3★ reviews the rest, and the 5★ count follows from the star sum. Shopify stores on HelpfulCrowd run on `shared/helpfulcrowd.ts` (`helpfulcrowd-pdp` / `-plp`, keyed by the store code its loader script carries): its widget serves five reviews a page, so a product reads its newest 50 and every list card fetches its own. OTTO serves its own reviews (`shared/otto.ts`): a product page carries its star histogram and reads up to `SAMPLE_MAX` reviews as HTML fragments of 30, and a list card reads its histogram off its product's reviews page past the last page of reviews. Printful serves its reviews from a CSRF-guarded RPC (`shared/printful.ts`): star-filtered counts give the score, pages of 50 the Sample, and a catalog card, which names its product only by slug, reads the id off the product page once.
- **variation dims** — `tallyVariationDims` (in `variation-table.ts`, beside `renderVariationCard`) folds a product's reviews into ranked `VarDim[]` per variation dimension (Colour, Size…) using the shared `starScore`; the per-site part is only how each review's `[dim, value]` pairs and rating are read (index-aligned for AliExpress, `transactionId`-keyed for Etsy).

## Deployment

Web is `truescore.mohamed3on.com` (Cloudflare → Hetzner). Pushes to `packages/web/**` or `packages/gmaps-shared/**` auto-deploy via `.github/workflows/deploy-web.yml`. The extension builds locally (`bun build.ts`) and ships separately. See `packages/web/deploy/HETZNER.md`.
